Game derivatives: drip-a.wav, drip-b.wav, drip-c.wav
Mono 44.1 kHz / 16-bit PCM; high-pass at 500 Hz, low-pass at 6500 Hz,
isolated faucet impacts, DC removal, 2 ms onset / 35 ms tail fades,
and peak matching to 0.65 for mixing headroom.
Crops: 0.714–0.954 s, 1.429–1.669 s, 3.579–3.819 s.
These capture the actual splash transients rather than the low tub/room
resonance between drops. No synthesized tones are mixed into the recordings.
No external audio requests are made during gameplay.
